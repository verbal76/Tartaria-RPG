should land nearly whole where the same content once lost 60,000-75,000 chars.

1. Part count. Complete (N/N) or short? mtaxtx2bzl8l is the open oddity — device

   on 1520 blocks it should be ~1,400-3,500.
3. Whether the issue walk keeps every listing ending SHORT of a full page, which
   is the only state that proves a MISSING part is genuinely absent.
