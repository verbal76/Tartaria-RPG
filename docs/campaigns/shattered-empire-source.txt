Campaign Title: The Shattered Empire of Tartaria
Campaign Overview:
This campaign takes place in the ancient, mud-ridden lands of Tartaria, where the players must navigate between factions, relics of an ancient empire, and the rise of the Mud Monarchs. The campaign focuses on exploration, diplomacy, and combat as the players seek to uncover and control the powerful Tartarian relics that could reshape the world.
Starting Level: 1Party Size: 3–6 playersGame System: Tartaria Prima Custom Ruleset
Campaign Length: 10–12 sessions, broken into 5 Acts. Each session should take about 3-4 hours.
Act 1: The Awakening Mud
Session 1: Into the Ruins
The players begin as explorers or mercenaries hired by various factions to uncover the secrets of Kholdyr, an ancient Tartarian ruin slowly resurfacing after centuries beneath the mud. The session focuses on the players gathering their first set of weapons and armor and making alliances with NPCs from their chosen factions.
Objectives:
Players meet NPCs from the Reclaimers, Forgotten Order, and Mud Dwellers factions.
Roll for starting attributes and Tartarian Coin (TC), per the rules for each race.
The party is tasked with retrieving an ancient Tartarian relic hidden in Kholdyr while evading hostile Mud Constructs.
Key Encounters:
Combat with Mud Golems and Mud Dwellers trying to stop the party.
Roleplay encounters with Dr. Melior Vast, a high-ranking member of the Forgotten Order, and Tareen Drez, a Reclaimer agent who offers rewards in exchange for Tartarian tech.
Session 2: Beneath the Mud
As the players explore Kholdyr, they uncover signs of the Mud Monarchs—ancient rulers of Tartaria who used powerful etheric technology to bend the world to their will. The players must navigate the shifting, unstable ruins while uncovering more clues about the Monarchs’ return.
Objectives:
Retrieve the Etheric Compass, an ancient device needed to navigate Tartaria’s deep ruins.
Avoid mudslides and collapsing tunnels within the ruins.
Key Encounters:
Combat with Mud Golems protecting the inner sanctum.
An encounter with a Mud Monarch Herald, warning the party of the Monarchs' return.
Act 2: Faction Wars
Session 3: Choices of Allegiance
After retrieving the Etheric Compass, the players return to Karhold, a major settlement on the outskirts of the Tartarian ruins. Here, they must decide which faction to support, which will shape the campaign's trajectory.
Objectives:
Choose a faction to align with: Reclaimers, Forgotten Order, or Mud Dwellers.
Each faction offers different rewards: Reclaimers offer weapons and technology, the Forgotten Order offers arcane knowledge, and the Mud Dwellers provide access to secret Tartarian pathways.
Session 4: Rising Tensions
The chosen faction sends the players on a mission to secure a key Tartarian outpost, Vorgor Keep, that has recently been discovered. However, rival factions vie for control, leading to tense negotiations or potential conflict.
Objectives:
Take control of Vorgor Keep before opposing factions can.
Engage in diplomacy or combat with rival factions, depending on the players' choices.
Act 3: The Rise of the Mud Monarchs
Session 5: The Monarch's Return
The players witness the full return of the Mud Monarchs as King Abzul emerges from the mud, seeking to reclaim his ancient throne. The players must stop Abzul from completing a ritual that will plunge the land into eternal darkness.
Key Encounters:
Combat with Mud Monarch Lieutenants and their etheric-powered warriors.
Navigate through a collapsing underground temple as the Mud Monarch’s power grows.
Structure Format Notes:
Objectives: Clearly outlined objectives for each session.
Key NPCs: Introduced characters who will impact the story.
Encounters: Modular combat, puzzle, or social interactions for each session.
Faction Choice: Choices throughout the campaign that impact the world and character relationships.
Dynamic Encounters: Balancing between exploration, combat, and role-playing for player engagement.
Act 3: The Rise of the Mud Monarchs (continued)
Session 6: The Battle for Vorgor Keep
With Vorgor Keep under siege, the players must hold the line against the Mud Monarch’s forces. The faction they aligned with in previous sessions will determine the reinforcements they receive and the approach they take.
Objectives:
Defend Vorgor Keep from waves of Mud Constructs and Mud Monarch’s Lieutenants.
Make strategic use of Tartarian Relics or Etheric Weapons found in the keep to turn the tide of battle.
Key Encounters:
Mud Siege Breakers: Massive golem-like creatures that lead the charge. Defeating them is key to survival.
Final Wave: The players must face the Herald of King Abzul, a powerful foe with etheric abilities that challenge the party’s strength and coordination.
Act 4: Secrets Beneath the Surface
Session 7: Into the Heart of Tartaria
The players receive information that King Abzul plans to complete an ancient ritual in the ruins of Ghor’tar, a long-buried Tartarian capital. They must venture deep into the underground tunnels to stop him, using the Etheric Compass to navigate the shifting mud corridors.
Objectives:
Reach Ghor’tar before King Abzul completes his ritual.
Navigate dangerous tunnels filled with Mud Spirits, ancient guardians of the buried city.
Key Encounters:
Mud Elementals: Creatures born of Tartaria’s corrupted soil, immune to standard weapons but vulnerable to etheric magic.
Puzzle Challenge: The players must solve a series of ancient Tartarian puzzles to deactivate the mud traps protecting the heart of Ghor’tar.
Session 8: The Monarch’s Chamber
Deep within Ghor’tar, the players discover the Monarch’s Chamber, where King Abzul prepares to merge his essence with the Heart of Tartaria, a powerful relic capable of reshaping the land. The players must confront him before the ritual is complete.
Objectives:
Stop King Abzul from completing the ritual.
Destroy or deactivate the Heart of Tartaria, deciding whether to use its power or seal it away forever.
Key Encounters:
King Abzul: A climactic battle against the Mud Monarch himself, using the Heart of Tartaria to summon waves of mud creatures and unleash devastating etheric energy blasts.
Choice-Based Ending: The players must decide what to do with the Heart after the battle. Destroying it might end the Mud Monarchs' influence for good, but using it could grant immense power at the cost of becoming targets for the Forgotten Order or the Reclaimers.
Act 5: The Shattered Throne
Session 9: The Aftermath
With the Mud Monarchs defeated (or sealed), the players must deal with the aftermath of their decisions. Depending on how they handled the Heart of Tartaria, different factions will react:
Reclaimers: If the players choose to hand over the relics, the Reclaimers seek to restore Tartaria’s former glory, but the power may corrupt some.
Forgotten Order: If the players aligned with the Forgotten Order, they might attempt to use the relics for more arcane, mysterious purposes, possibly restarting the cycle of ruin.
Mud Dwellers: The players may face consequences for siding with the Mud Dwellers, who want to keep Tartaria's power hidden from the world.
Key Encounters:
Political intrigue and final skirmishes with factions that oppose the players’ choices.
A showdown with remnants of the Mud Monarch’s followers who refuse to accept defeat.
Session 10: The Final Choice
The campaign concludes as the players prepare for one final decision: How will they leave Tartaria? Do they take the knowledge and power they’ve acquired to their faction? Do they stay and rebuild the lost empire? Or do they leave Tartaria’s secrets buried forever?
Objectives:
The players must choose their legacy: reclaim Tartaria for their faction, destroy its remaining relics, or rule as monarchs.
Key Encounters:
A final roleplay-heavy session, where the players’ decisions shape the world's future.
Optional epilogue combat if a faction seeks to claim Tartaria by force after the players’ choice.
Optional Epilogue: Rise of a New Empire
Should the players choose to seize control of Tartaria for themselves or their faction, an epilogue can be run that deals with the consequences of ruling over the remnants of the lost empire. They will face external threats, from neighboring kingdoms to the rise of new powers within Tartaria itself. This epilogue can set the stage for a future campaign.
Campaign NPCs:
Here’s a recap of key NPCs the players will encounter throughout the campaign:
Dr. Melior Vast – A scholar from the Forgotten Order seeks to control Tartaria’s relics for research.
Tareen Drez – A Reclaimer who wants to restore Tartaria’s tech for the betterment of society but may have ulterior motives.
King Abzul – The ancient Mud Monarch who seeks to reclaim his throne and control the Heart of Tartaria.
Herald of King Abzul – The Mud Monarch’s right-hand lieutenant, sent to stop the players from disrupting their plans.
Lira Valen – Leader of the Mud Dwellers who opposes the reclamation of Tartarian power by outsiders.
Final Notes:
This campaign provides a modular, adaptable storyline that can evolve based on the players’ choices. The campaign can lead to different endings depending on the faction they support and their decisions regarding Tartaria’s power. The optional epilogue allows for future campaigns, giving the world of Tartaria Prima continued depth and complexity.
