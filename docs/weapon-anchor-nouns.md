# TARTARIA WEAPON NAME GENERATOR — ANCHOR NOUN LIBRARY

Purpose: terminal/head nouns for procedural weapon names.
Pattern: [modifier] [modifier] [ANCHOR NOUN]
Examples: Rust-Marked Knife | Grave Iron Maul | Plasma Bolt-Caster

Design notes:
- Exactly 50 anchors per category.
- Existing Tartaria Armoury terminology is preserved where useful, then extended with setting-compatible vocabulary.
- These are weapon-type/head nouns, not complete generated names.
- Ranged vocabulary intentionally preserves Tartaria's mixed firearm terminology rather than forcing every gun into Bolt-Caster.
- Natural = biological/anatomical weapon forms.
- Agent = weaponized substances, payloads, contaminants, energetic agents, or delivery compounds.

## GENERATOR RULES

1. Select exactly one anchor noun from the category appropriate to the weapon.
2. Place the anchor noun at the end of the generated weapon name.
3. Add one or two modifiers before the anchor noun.
4. Do not turn descriptors such as Rust-Marked, Plasma, Bone, Aetheric, Grave, Ancient, or Military into the terminal noun unless they are explicitly present in an anchor above.
5. Prefer short names. Two or three total lexical units is the normal target.
6. Hyphenated anchor nouns such as Bolt-Caster, Cross-Saber, Shield-Hammer, Magna-Cannon, and Aether-Lance count as one anchor.
7. Preserve category semantics: a Blade anchor should read as a cutting weapon; Blunt as impact; Polearm as long-reach; Ranged as projectile/energy; Natural as anatomical/biological; Agent as a harmful substance or payload.
8. Do not automatically modernize Tartaria terminology. Rifle, Cannon, Carbine, Railgun, Bolt-Caster, Bow, and Crossbow may coexist.
