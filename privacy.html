Tartaria Realms — Privacy Policy

Effective: May 31, 2026

The short version

Tartaria Realms is a local, single-player text RPG. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party marketing SDKs. Your save data, character history, and inventory exist only on your device.

What the app does on your device

  • Stores your game progress, characters, and settings locally using your device's standard app storage.
  • Runs all narrative generation, lore lookup, and speech synthesis on-device, using small AI models bundled with the app.
  • Plays audio, accepts taps and (optionally) voice input, and renders the game.

What the app sends over the network

The app makes a small number of outbound network requests, listed here for transparency. None of these requests carry personal information about you, and the developer does not receive any user-identifying data from them.

  • Over-the-air (OTA) updates. When the app launches, it checks Expo's update service (u.expo.dev) to see whether a new version of the game's content is available. As with any web request, Expo's servers may record standard request metadata such as IP address and timestamp; the developer does not access these logs. See Expo's privacy policy at https://expo.dev/privacy.
  • AI model downloads (first launch only). The first time the app runs, it downloads its on-device AI models (small language model and embedding model used for narration and lore lookup) from Expo's content delivery network. These downloads are anonymous; no account or identity is associated with them.

Microphone and voice input

If you choose to use voice input, the app uses your device's built-in speech-recognition service to convert your speech to text. On iOS, this is handled by Apple's Speech framework and is governed by Apple's privacy practices; on Android, by Google's on-device or cloud-based speech service depending on your device settings. Voice audio is processed by the operating system, not by the app developer, and is never sent to the developer's servers (we don't have any). See Apple's privacy policy at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. Voice input is entirely optional; the game is fully playable by tap.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is no special handling required for children's data. Parents and guardians may install, uninstall, and configure the app freely; deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

Third parties

The app uses no advertising networks, no analytics services, and no marketing or attribution SDKs. The only third-party services the app interacts with are the OTA / model-distribution endpoint operated by Expo (described above) and, when applicable, your device's built-in operating-system services (such as Apple's Speech framework). The developer has no agreements with these services that would cause them to share data with us, and we receive none.

Data retention and deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. To delete it, simply uninstall the app — your device's normal app-uninstall process removes the game's saves, settings, and downloaded models. The developer has no remote copy of your data and therefore has nothing to delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published at this URL with an updated effective date. Material changes (for example, if a future version of the app ever did collect personal data) would be disclosed in-app on first launch of the updated version.
